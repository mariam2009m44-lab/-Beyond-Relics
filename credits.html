Transparency · Sources · Provenance

Credits & methodology.

Beyond Relics is an independent educational project. This page explains where mission information and visual material come from, and how the project distinguishes historical evidence from interpretation.

Visual fidelity labels

  • Official / Scan — an original official scan or source asset, linked to its source record.
  • Official model, simplified — an official model adapted for performance, with the source identified.
  • Our reconstruction — a project-built reconstruction whose reference sources are listed.
  • Artistic visualization — an interpretive visual, not a scale-accurate model or official engineering representation.

Interactive artifact models currently use procedural geometry and are labeled Artistic visualization. Their appearance is explanatory, not evidence of exact hardware dimensions or construction.

Scientific sources

Each artifact record links to primary mission sources in its detail page. The collection data is maintained in the artifact records. NASA image search results link to individual NASA archive records; verify the source and credit for each selected image.

Measurements and plotted values must be tied to the exact dataset, instrument, units, and processing method. A diagram or reconstructed visualization must never be described as raw mission telemetry.

Software and design dependencies

  • Three.js — interactive 3D rendering library (MIT License).
  • Google Fonts — Barlow Condensed, Cormorant Garamond, and DM Sans (SIL Open Font License).
  • NASA Image and Video Library — dynamically retrieved imagery; check each individual record for creator credits and restrictions.

Asset register and open checks

The complete working register lists visual assets, dependencies, and unresolved provenance items. Some local story-panel authorship and reuse permissions still require confirmation; those entries are explicitly marked unverified rather than assigned an assumed license.

AI and reconstruction disclosure

Procedural 3D scenes are educational reconstructions. Do not interpret them as official NASA scans or engineering CAD. Any AI-generated visual content must be identified and handled according to the relevant challenge rules; NASA logos, flags, and mission identifiers must not be invented or generated as if official.
