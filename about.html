WHY THIS ARCHIVE EXISTS

The mission behind
the machines.

Beyond Relics is an educational space archive about the human-made hardware that remains on the Moon, Mars, and throughout deep space.

More than forgotten equipment

Every lander, rover, instrument, and probe represents years of imagination, engineering, teamwork, and discovery. Some completed their planned missions. Others continue to send signals or operate far beyond their original design lifetimes.

This project invites students and curious explorers to learn what these machines were built to do, what they discovered, and why their stories still matter.

Explore with curiosity

Use the artifact archive to meet individual machines, follow the timeline to connect missions across decades, and read the cinematic story to experience exploration through a visual journey.

Educational note: mission facts and dates should be checked against official mission sources before publication.
